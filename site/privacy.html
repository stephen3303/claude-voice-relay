Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

In short

With your own relay, Bridge has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Bridge never sends your audio anywhere; see Your voice for Apple's speech recognition. What you say and the photos you take go only to the relay on your own PC, over your own Tailscale network. The developer never receives them. When the app crashes or something fails, it sends a crash or error report so it can be fixed; see Crash and error reports.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Bridge iPhone app when it is connected to your own relay, and the open-source relay itself, published by the developer of Bridge ("I", "me"). It does not cover Bridge Cloud, the Bridge account (Sign in with Apple) or the Bridge connector for Claude; this policy will be updated to cover them before they are offered.

What Bridge does not collect

  • No account: you never sign up or sign in to Bridge.
  • No analytics, no ads and no tracking, in the app or on this website. Crash and error reports, below, are not analytics and are not linked to you. This website has no cookies or scripts; it is hosted by GitHub Pages, which handles its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • I never receive your audio, the text of what you say, your photos, or the answers you hear.

Your voice

Bridge turns speech into text using Apple's speech recognition. Where your iPhone supports on-device recognition for your language, audio never leaves the iPhone. Otherwise Apple's servers recognise it, under Apple's privacy policy. Bridge itself never sends your audio anywhere.

Text and photos

The recognised text, and any photo you take with the glasses camera, go only to the relay running on your own PC, over your Tailscale network. Photos are deleted from the PC once they have been answered.

Claude Code and Anthropic

On your PC, the relay runs Claude Code, which sends your prompts and photos to Anthropic under your own Claude account. That processing is governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy, not by me.

Optional natural voice (OpenAI and Amazon Polly)

Natural voice is off by default. If you turn it on in the app's settings and have added your own OpenAI API key to the relay on your PC, the relay sends the text of each answer to OpenAI to turn it into speech, under your own OpenAI account and OpenAI's terms and privacy policy. Otherwise answers are spoken by your iPhone and nothing goes to OpenAI.

With Bridge Cloud and Bridge Pro, turning on Natural voice sends the text of each answer to Amazon Polly, on Bridge's Amazon Web Services account, to turn it into speech. Bridge Cloud counts the characters for a daily limit but doesn't store the text. With Natural voice off, nothing goes to Polly.

Crash and error reports

When the app crashes, freezes, or a step fails (for example the glasses camera or a call to your relay), it sends a report to Sentry, a crash reporting service, under Sentry's privacy policy. A report holds the error, where in the app it happened, the app version, and the iPhone's model, iOS version and state (such as free memory and battery). It holds no name, account, audio, text of what you say, photos, answers, PC address or relay token, and it is not linked to you. Sentry is set not to store IP addresses. Reports are used only to fix Bridge.

Ray-Ban Meta glasses

The Meta AI app connects the glasses to your iPhone, under Meta's terms. Bridge opts out of the analytics and crash reporting in the Meta Wearables toolkit it uses.

Your relay token

The token Bridge uses to reach your relay is stored in the iPhone Keychain.

Payments

Bridge Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no payment details.

Children

Bridge collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
