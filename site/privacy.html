Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

In short

Bridge has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your audio stays on your iPhone. What you say and the photos you take go only to the relay on your own PC, over your own Tailscale network. The developer never receives them.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Bridge iPhone app and the open-source relay it talks to, published by Stephen Smitherman ("I", "me").

It does not yet cover Bridge Cloud or the Bridge connector for Claude, which use a Bridge account and are not available yet. This policy will be updated to cover them before they launch.

What Bridge does not collect

  • No account: you never sign up or sign in to Bridge.
  • No analytics, no ads and no tracking, in the app or on this website. This website has no cookies or scripts; it is hosted by GitHub Pages, which handles its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • I never receive your audio, the text of what you say, your photos, or the answers you hear.

Your voice

Bridge turns speech into text on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio never leaves the iPhone.

Text and photos

The recognised text, and any photo you take with the glasses camera, go only to the relay running on your own PC, over your Tailscale network. Photos are deleted from the PC once they have been answered.

Claude Code and Anthropic

On your PC, the relay runs Claude Code, which sends your prompts and photos to Anthropic under your own Claude account. That processing is governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy, not by me.

Optional natural voice (OpenAI)

If you add your own OpenAI API key to the relay on your PC, the relay sends the text of each answer to OpenAI to turn it into speech, under your own OpenAI account and OpenAI's terms and privacy policy. Without a key, answers are spoken by your iPhone and nothing goes to OpenAI.

Ray-Ban Meta glasses

The Meta AI app connects the glasses to your iPhone, under Meta's terms. Bridge opts out of the analytics and crash reporting in the Meta Wearables toolkit it uses.

Your relay token

The token Bridge uses to reach your relay is stored in the iPhone Keychain.

Payments

Bridge Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no payment details.

Children

Bridge collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
