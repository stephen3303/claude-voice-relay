Bridge

Talk to your Claude Code agents hands-free, through Ray-Ban Meta glasses, your headphones, or any Claude app.

Coming soon to the App Store

Where it works

  • Any Claude app

    Add Bridge as a connector in Claude, and Claude can start tasks for your agents and read their answers. Connectors sync across Claude on the web, desktop and mobile, so it isn't tied to one computer or phone.

    Coming with Bridge Cloud.

  • The Bridge iPhone app

    The hands-free part: talk through Ray-Ban Meta glasses or any Bluetooth headset, take photos with the glasses camera, and let agents call you when they have something to say.

    Coming soon to the App Store.

  • Self-hosted on your PC

    Run the open-source relay on your own Windows PC, and your agents run there in Docker, one per project, reached over your private Tailscale network.

    Available now. Setup is in the README.

How it works

With the iPhone app and the self-hosted relay:

  1. 1

    You speak

    Bridge turns your speech into text on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio stays on the phone.

  2. 2

    It goes to your PC

    The text travels over your private Tailscale connection to the open-source relay on your PC. The relay runs Claude Code agents in Docker containers, one per project.

  3. 3

    You hear the answer

    The agent's answer is spoken back to you. While a conversation is open, replies are read aloud as they arrive.

What you can say

  • Start a task

    Say "in <project>, <task>". Bridge reads the task back before it's sent; say "send it" to confirm.

  • Answer, check, hang up

    "Yes" answers an agent's question. "Status" tells you where things are. "Goodbye" hangs up.

  • Show Claude what you see

    Say "look at this, …" and the glasses camera takes a photo; you hear Claude's answer. Say "send this to <project>, …" to give the photo to that project's agent. Photos are deleted from the PC once answered.

  • Start it your way

    Use Siri ("Talk to Bridge"), the Action Button, or Shortcuts. Shortcuts actions: Talk to Bridge, Ask Bridge and Look with Bridge.

What you need

  • For the connector: Claude on any device, and a Bridge account (Sign in with Apple).
  • For hands-free voice: an iPhone running iOS 17.2 or later, and any Bluetooth headset. Ray-Ban Meta glasses add the camera features.
  • To self-host: a Windows PC running the relay, with Docker and Tailscale, and your own Claude subscription signed in to Claude Code on that PC.

Questions

Do I need a Windows PC?

Only to self-host. The relay on your own PC is one way to run your agents; the connector and Bridge Cloud don't need one.

Does my voice leave my iPhone?

No. Speech is recognised on the iPhone. Only the recognised text is sent on: to the relay on your own PC over your Tailscale network, or to Bridge Cloud if you use it.

Do I need an account?

Not to self-host: with the relay on your PC there is no account, and it uses your own Claude subscription, signed in to Claude Code on that PC. The connector and Bridge Cloud use a Bridge account, created with Sign in with Apple. Either way there are no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Do I need Ray-Ban Meta glasses?

Only for the camera features. Voice conversations work with any Bluetooth headset.

What runs on my PC if I self-host?

The relay, which is open source. It runs Claude Code agents in Docker containers, one per project. Setup is in the README.
