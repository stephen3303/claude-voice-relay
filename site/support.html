Support

Set up Bridge, fix common problems, and get help.

Setting up

These steps are for self-hosting, with the relay on your own Windows PC. Today the relay is the only way to run your agents. The Bridge connector for Claude is coming with Bridge Cloud, which won't need a PC. To self-host:

  1. On your PC, install Docker and Tailscale, and sign in to Claude Code with your own Claude subscription.
  2. Install and start the relay. The steps are in the README on GitHub.
  3. On your iPhone, turn on Tailscale and join the same network as your PC.
  4. In Bridge, under PC relay, enter your relay's address and token, then tap Save and test.
  5. For the camera features, tap Connect to Meta AI, then Allow camera.

Troubleshooting

Bridge can't reach my PC

  • Check that Tailscale is on, on both your iPhone and your PC.
  • Check that the relay is running on your PC.
  • In Bridge, tap Save and test and read the result shown below it.

Photos don't work

  • Open Bridge and tap Allow camera.
  • Photos are part of Bridge Pro.

I don't hear the agent's answer

  • Replies are read aloud while a conversation is open. Start one with Siri ("Talk to Bridge"), the Action Button, or Shortcuts.
  • Say "status" to hear where things are.

Managing a subscription

Cancel or change Bridge Pro any time in iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Please don't include your relay token or address.
